Q: How do you win at backgammon?
A: Move all 15 of your checkers around the board into your home board
   (points 1–6) and bear them off before your opponent does. Qavex
   Backgammon Gold enforces these official rules automatically, including
   hitting blots, re-entering from the bar, and gammon/backgammon scoring.

Q: What is the doubling cube in backgammon?
A: The doubling cube raises the stakes of a game from 1 point up to 64.
   Either player may offer to double before rolling; the opponent may
   accept (taking ownership of the cube) or decline (losing immediately).
   Qavex Backgammon Gold implements the full offer/accept/decline flow,
   and its AI opponents know when to double.

Q: What is the Crawford rule?
A: In match play, when a player is one point from winning the match, the
   doubling cube is retired for the next game. Qavex Backgammon Gold
   applies the Crawford rule automatically in the final round of the
   Tournament Ladder.

Q: What is a gammon and a backgammon?
A: A gammon (double game) is won when the loser has not borne off any
   checker — it counts double. A backgammon (triple game) is won when the
   loser still has a checker on the bar or in the winner's home board —
   it counts triple. The app detects and scores both automatically.

Q: Can I play backgammon offline without ads?
A: Yes. Qavex Backgammon Gold is fully offline with no advertisements.
   The only in-app purchase is an optional $0.99 "Buy Me a Coffee" tip
   that never gates gameplay.

Q: Is backgammon good for beginners?
A: Yes — the rules take minutes to learn. The app includes a How to Play
   guide, a Practice mode vs Easy AI with hints always on, and a Kids
   Mode with bigger text and no doubling cube.
