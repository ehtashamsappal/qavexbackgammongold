Qavex Backgammon Gold ("the App") is developed by muhammad ehtasham
under the QAVEX brand (https://www.qavex.company/). Your privacy
matters. This policy explains what the App does — and does not do —
with your information.

1. NO DATA COLLECTION
The App collects no personal data. It does not ask for your name,
email, phone number, location, contacts, or any other personal
information. There is no account, no sign-in, and no server that
receives your data.

2. ON-DEVICE STORAGE ONLY
Game progress, statistics, achievements, settings, themes, and your
Doubloon Coins balance are stored locally on your device using the
operating system's standard app storage. This data never leaves your
device. Deleting the App deletes this data.

3. NO ADVERTISING, NO TRACKERS
The App contains no advertisements and no third-party analytics,
tracking, or advertising SDKs.

4. IN-APP PURCHASE
The App offers a single optional in-app purchase: "Buy Me a Coffee"
($0.99, product ID: qavexbackgammongoldcoffee). Purchases are processed
entirely by the Google Play Store under Google's own privacy policy.
QAVEX does not receive or store your payment details. The purchase is
a voluntary tip and never gates gameplay.

5. PERMISSIONS
The App requests no sensitive device permissions. It works fully
offline and requires no network access for gameplay.

6. CHILDREN
The App is suitable for general audiences and includes a Kids Mode
(bigger text, automatic hints, no doubling cube). Because the App
collects no data from anyone, there is nothing to collect from
children either.

7. CHANGES TO THIS POLICY
If this policy changes, the updated version will be published with
the App's store listing. Continued use of the App after changes take
effect constitutes acceptance.
